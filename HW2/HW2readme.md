# Homework Question:

Write a Python Program that calculates how much someone will spend at a movie theater. The program should ask the user their age, whether it is the weekend, whether they are a member of the theater, whether they want popcorn.

## Use the following rules

- Children under 13 pay 6 dollars
- People 13 through 64 pay 12 dollars
- People over 65 pay 9 dollars
- Weekend tickets cost 4 more dollars
- Theater members receive 2 dollar discount
- An age below 0 or above 120 is invalid
- Popcorn costs 6 dollars

I would recommend doing two separate functions for ticket price and popcorn. Final price displayed with two decimals.

## Explanation:

This problem assesses a student’s ability to use functions, parameters, returned values, conditional statements, Boolean values, and basic input validation. They must work through different discounts of prices and certain values in order to put together final prices. This puts together multiple topics we have already learned this year.

The most challenging part will likely be applying the different age conditions in the proper order while also applying weekend charge and membership discount. This will help students put two functions together.
